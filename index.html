📋 About The Nepal Desk — and how it works

The Nepal Desk is a free Nepal news aggregator built for two audiences at once: readers in Nepal, and the millions of Nepalis who live abroad and still start their morning with news from home. It tracks top Nepal headlines, Kathmandu politics and policy (parliament, elections, government, the economy), the Nepali diaspora (non-resident Nepalis, students, migrant workers and community news from America, Australia, the Gulf, the UK and beyond), and Nepali-language headlines (नेपाल समाचार) straight from Nepal's own newsrooms. Twice an hour, a wire scan collects the latest headlines from the public feeds of The Kathmandu Post, Online Khabar, Ratopati, NepalNews and Nagarik, plus Google News searches for Nepal and the diaspora, removes duplicates, and files them by desk.

Method, in the open: headlines and short snippets belong to their publishers — every card names its source, shows its publication time, and links to the original story. Nothing here is republished in full, and no headline is rewritten. Developing stories should always be confirmed at the source. The desk is edited by Bishow Pandey (@bishowppandey), a senior software & platform engineer from Nepal, now in Kentucky, USA, who also runs The World Desk (world news) and The Dev Desk (developer news).

Frequently asked questions

What is The Nepal Desk? A free Nepal-news wire aggregating top headlines, politics and policy, diaspora news and Nepali-language headlines from established Nepali outlets' public feeds, updated twice an hour.

Where does the news come from? The Kathmandu Post, Online Khabar, Ratopati, NepalNews and Nagarik — public RSS feeds, no logins, no paywall bypass — plus Google News searches for the diaspora. Every headline links to its source.

How fresh is it? The scan runs twice an hour; each headline carries its own publication time so you can judge for yourself.

Who runs it? Bishow Pandey — follow him on X at @bishowppandey for the live wire between updates.
